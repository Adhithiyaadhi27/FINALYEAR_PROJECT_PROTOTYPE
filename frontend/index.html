    11. Limitations & Medical Disclaimer

    Not a medical device. This is a research/academic software prototype. It does not measure blood pressure clinically, does not diagnose hypertension or hypotension, and does not recommend medication changes. Always confirm with a validated cuff and a qualified healthcare professional. Results may not generalise beyond the single public dataset used for offline evaluation.

    Phase 1 restrictions: no Arduino/ESP32/Raspberry Pi sensors, no live streaming, no wearables, no clinical deployment.
